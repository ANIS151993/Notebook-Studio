Scientific Basis and Research Objective

Problem framing, hypotheses, and measurable research goals.

Research Problem

Raw CSV workflows are often non-reproducible, labor-intensive, and error-prone due to inconsistent schemas, duplicate rows, missing values, and fragmented multi-tool processing.

Core Hypothesis

A guided automation-first architecture with browser-native execution and deterministic repair logic can reduce preprocessing burden while improving repeatability and debugging reliability.

Primary Objectives

  • Build deterministic CSV cleaning + notebook generation pipeline.
  • Evaluate productivity gain versus manual baseline workflows.
  • Assess error recovery behavior under hybrid rule/model repair strategy.

Applied Domains

  • Academic labs and reproducible coursework analytics.
  • SME operations teams without dedicated data engineering staff.
  • Analyst pipelines for anomaly/fraud readiness screening.

DataMentor: A Reproducible Framework for Serverless CSV Intelligence, Offline Local Language-Model Repair, and Interactive Notebook Analytics

Md Anisur Rahman Chowdhury

Department of Computer and Information Science, Gannon University, USA

Abstract

DataMentor transforms heterogeneous CSV datasets into reproducible notebook workflows with deterministic preprocessing, in-browser execution, and profile-based analytics evaluation.

The framework combines measurable quality uplift with strong runtime continuity through a rule-first correction pipeline and local-model fallback.

Highlights

Evaluation reports substantial workflow-time reduction across academic, SME, and enterprise profiles, with robust Python correction performance under offline deployment constraints.
